Frequently Asked Questions

Will renaming a Google Form question break my choice limits?

No. FormCap binds every rule to the question’s immutable Google Form Item ID rather than its title text.

Does FormCap preserve Go-to-Section branching?

Yes. Whenever choices are updated, hidden, or annotated with seats-left badges, FormCap preserves each option’s page navigation rule.

What happens if two respondents submit the last spot at the exact same second?

Google Forms does not live-refresh open respondent tabs. FormCap serializes updates using Apps Script locks and immediately flags any millisecond race in the sidebar Overbooking Alert banner so you see it right away.

How do I manage or cancel my Pro subscription?

Click the green PRO pill in the FormCap sidebar and choose Manage Subscription (Stripe Portal) to cancel or update billing anytime. Need help? Email sdai.code@gmail.com.
